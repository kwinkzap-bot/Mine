    Filters every F&O stock — no symbol selection needed. Scans candles on the selected Timeframe, over Start Date → End Date, and lists every candle that touched, then closed beyond that stock's current monthly-expiry-cycle High (BUY) or Low (SELL) — a genuine touch (the candle's range straddles the level), not a clean gap-through — AND whose close also clears every EMA 20/50/100/200 on the same Timeframe (above all for BUY, below all for SELL). EMA Filter additionally requires the candle to have touched at least one of those EMAs, touched none of them, or (Touch & Not touch) either way. Expiry day is auto-detected (last Thursday through Aug-2025, last Tuesday from Sep-2025 onward); a holiday snaps back to the previous trading day.
    30-Min Opening Fakeout — Rule
    SELL / BUY setup, targets & cut-off behaviour

    Filters every F&O stock — no symbol selection needed (indices excluded, since there's no equity share of an index to buy). Every symbol is sized as a real intraday (MIS) equity position — quantity = capital ÷ price × 5x leverage, same as your broker's order-entry quantity field. Every day, using 30-min candles from 09:15:

    SELL setup (breakdown)

    1. Candle 1 is green
    2. Candle 2 is green with its High above Candle 1's High, and its close above Candle 1's High
    3. Candle 3 crosses above Candle 2's High but closes back below it (fakeout)
    4. Candle 3's close stays at/above Candle 2's 50% level (midpoint of its High/Low)
    5. Candle 3's lower wick ≤ upper wick
    6. If Candle 3 is green (against SELL), its body ≤ 1/4 of its High-Low range

    Trigger = Candle 3's Low − 0.05% — a break below it fires SELL
    SL = Candle 3's real High  ·  Target = day's session Low

    BUY setup (breakout) — mirror

    1. Candle 1 is red
    2. Candle 2 is red with its Low below Candle 1's Low, and its close below Candle 1's Low
    3. Candle 3 crosses below Candle 2's Low but closes back above it (fakeout)
    4. Candle 3's close stays at/below Candle 2's 50% level (midpoint of its High/Low)
    5. Candle 3's upper wick ≤ lower wick
    6. If Candle 3 is red (against BUY), its body ≤ 1/4 of its High-Low range

    Trigger = Candle 3's High + 0.05% — a break above it fires BUY
    SL = Candle 3's real Low  ·  Target = day's session High

    • The trigger adds a 0.05% buffer beyond Candle 3's Low/High — price must clear it, not just touch it. SL stays at the exact (unbuffered) Candle 3 High/Low. Toggle with Entry Buffer, on by default.
    • The Candle 3 against-direction body-size cap (¼ of range) — toggle with Body Filter, on by default.
    • Candle 2's close beyond Candle 1's High/Low (above Candle 1's High for SELL, below Candle 1's Low for BUY) — toggle with C2 Close > C1 High, on by default.
    • A trade whose sized SL risk (|entry − SL| × qty, at the current capital-per-trade) exceeds Max SL Risk (default ₹5,000, editable) is skipped entirely — toggle with SL Risk Filter, off by default.
    • Target (day's session Low/High) is only knowable in hindsight — a backtest-only level.
    • Force-closed at the Cut-off Time if neither SL nor Target is hit. SL/Target are confirmed the moment any bar's High/Low touches the level.
    • A flat ₹300 brokerage is deducted from every trade's P&L (₹).

    EMA Confluence Breakout — Rule
    4-EMA touch, High/Low breakout & targets

    Uses DAILY candles only, on the stock future picked in Symbol above — or, with All Stocks, on every stock in the per-stock defaults table at once, each with its own Direction/Target and sized by its own lot size. Four EMAs are tracked on the close: EMA 20, 50, 100, 200.

    Signal candle: its daily range must touch all four EMAs at once (Low ≤ EMA ≤ High, for EMA20, EMA50, EMA100 and EMA200 each). The candle's own color then fixes the direction and arms a breakout order off its own High/Low — watched on every later candle until it fills. A doji (close = open) gives no signal.

    BUY setup

    1. Signal candle is Green (close > open)
    2. Arms a breakout order at the signal candle's own High
    3. Fills once a later candle's High breaks above that level (at the level, or that candle's Open if it gapped through)

    SL = the signal candle's real Low
    Target = entry price + Target % (default 5%, min 1%)

    SELL setup — mirror

    1. Signal candle is Red (close < open)
    2. Arms a breakout order at the signal candle's own Low
    3. Fills once a later candle's Low breaks below that level (at the level, or that candle's Open if it gapped through)

    SL = the signal candle's real High
    Target = entry price − Target % (default 5%, min 1%)

    • Require R:R better than 1:1 (optional): with the checkbox on, a filled breakout is only taken when the Target sits further from entry than the SL does — Target distance > SL distance. SL is the signal candle's own opposite extreme, so a tall signal candle can risk more than the Target pays; those breakouts are skipped and the order is dropped (a later fill would only be a worse price), and the next confluence candle arms the next one.
    • Only one breakout order is armed at a time — while waiting for a fill, new signal candles are ignored. If price never breaks out, that signal never fills.
    • SL/Target are confirmed the moment any later daily bar's Low/High touches the level, starting from the breakout (entry) candle itself — SL is checked before Target within the same bar.
    • If the data ends while still in a trade, it is force-closed at the last available daily close.
    • Once a trade closes, scanning for the next signal resumes on the very next daily bar — any number of trades can occur across the date range.
    Sell one OTM CE and one OTM PE of the current weekly expiry at Entry Time · no target, buy back at Exit Time · each leg's stop is Stop Loss % above its own entry · a stopped leg is sold again once when its premium falls back to the original entry. Premiums are ICICI's recorded 1-minute candles (needs an ICICI login; run long ranges after market hours).
    Weekly CPR is narrow (≤ W CPR Width × its 10-week normal) → day opens inside it → first 1-hour candle (09:15–10:15) with range ≥ Min 1H Range % gives the signal: Any candle — any bearish (red) candle type = SELL, any bullish (green) one = BUY (Candle Type narrows it to one shape; a doji is neither); or 1H close outside CPR — closes below BC = SELL, above TC = BUY · SELL on a break BELOW that candle's Low, BUY on a break ABOVE its High, more than 0.5% short of weekly S3/R3 · SL = the other end · target = R × the candle's range · no break before the cut-off = no trade. Signals on the stock (Fyers); with Stock option basis the order is its ATM CE (BUY) / PE (SELL).
    Daily timeframe, positional — trades are carried overnight for days or weeks, with no intraday square-off. Daily Heikin-Ashi candles + SuperTrend. Entry: SuperTrend flips to your side within the last Fresh candles and the HA candle is your colour; the order fills the NEXT open. Stop: swing low (long) / high (short) of the last Swing candles. Target: 1:R sells the Partial share; the rest trails until SuperTrend flips back or N opposite HA candles print (exit next open), or the stop. Equity P&L: each trade is the Capital per trade, less the round-trip cost.
